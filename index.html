Our mission

A fully Danish-owned and operated company, delivering precision to those who defend us.

We are designing and prototyping an artillery rocket guidance kit that answers critical challenges emerging from the war in Europe.

Range−

Meeting critical range and accuracy requirements.

Unguided rockets scatter at long range. A guidance kit lets existing rockets reach much further and land where we want them to.

Crew+

Protecting launcher crews, an often-overlooked need.

Fewer salvos and longer stand-off distances mean less time exposed at the launch position.

Supply+

Fewer rockets per effect, easing shortages of legacy systems.

With our guidance kit, the same ammunition stockpile goes further. This matters when ammunition is scarce.

What we offer

Introducing DLLR-MARS-GK

Transforming legacy rocket artillery into precision strike assets.

A guidance kit fitted to rockets already in inventory, such as the 9M22S, adding range and accuracy to an already existing platform.

Adaptable

To fit multiple existing platforms.

Our platform integrates with a range of existing launch systems and can be deployed from vehicle-mounted launchers or from ground-based launch positions.

Advanced Guidance

Pinpoint accuracy in GPS-denied tactical environments.

Navigation and guidance that hold accuracy where satellite signals are jammed, spoofed or unavailable.
